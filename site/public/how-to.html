How to use PushArmor

1. Install

Get the Android app, the iPhone app, the Chrome extension or the Firefox add-on from the download page. Each install is one peer.

2. Sign in

Sign in with an email code, with Google or with Apple. Use the same email address on every peer, so all your peers join one account.

3. Pair your peers

On one peer, tap Pair next to the other peer. Then open PushArmor on the other peer.

Both peers show a Pairing fingerprint. If it is the same on both, tap Approve on both. If it is not the same, tap Deny.

Peers stay paired until you remove one.

4. Send

Share a link or text to PushArmor, or use the PushArmor menu in your browser. Then pick a peer, or pick All to send to all your paired peers.

To update the list of peers, tap Refresh.

More answers are on the support page.
