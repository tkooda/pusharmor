How to use PushArmor

1. Install

Get the Android app, the iPhone app, the Chrome extension, or the Firefox add-on from the download page. Each install counts as one device.

2. Sign in

Sign in with an email code, with Google, or with Apple. Use the same email address on every device, so all your devices join one account.

3. Pair your devices

On one device, tap Pair next to the other device. Then open PushArmor on that other device.

Both devices show a Pairing fingerprint. This is a short check code; if it matches on both screens, the pairing is safe. If it is the same on both, tap Approve on both. If it is not the same, tap Deny.

Devices stay paired until you remove one or delete your account.

4. Send

Share a link or a note to the PushArmor app, or use the PushArmor menu in your browser. Then choose which devices to send it to.
