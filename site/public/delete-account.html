Delete your PushArmor account

This page deletes the PushArmor account of an email address. You can also use Delete account in the app.

Deleted at once: all your devices, with their keys, names and the addresses they use to receive notifications (push tokens). Kept: a one-way code made from your email address (a keyed hash) with its ban flag, for banned accounts only, so a banned address stays banned; undelivered pushes at the vendors, up to 7 days (pairing requests up to 4 weeks); copies on your other devices. No login to this site is needed.
