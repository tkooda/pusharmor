Privacy policy

PushArmor is operated by Ion Echo, LLC at pusharmor.com.

We never sell or share your data with anyone for their own purposes; the services below process it for us.

Services we use

  • AWS SES: used for email sign-in only, to send the sign-in code. It sees your email address at send time. If a code email bounces, the mail provider keeps that address on a suppression list until we clear it.
  • Google's and Apple's notification services (FCM, APNs) for Android and iPhone, Google and Mozilla web push (Chrome, Firefox), and a UnifiedPush distributor if you chose one: used to wake your device. They see the address your device uses to receive notifications (push token or endpoint) and the encrypted message only. They hold undelivered messages up to 7 days (pairing requests up to 4 weeks).
  • Cloudflare: runs our relay server and its database, so it can technically see everything our relay server sees: your email address briefly at sign-in (to send the code), your IP address, device public keys, push tokens and encrypted messages. Cloudflare also keeps our delivery logs for 7 days: account id, device ids and send results; no content, no IP, no email. Cloudflare's bot check (Turnstile) runs on the email sign-in page and on the delete-account page only.
  • Google and Apple sign-in (if you choose them): used to prove the account. We keep only a one-way code made from your email address (a keyed hash), which is your account id (see below).

Your email address

We do not store your email address. We keep only a one-way code made from it, which cannot be turned back into the address.

Why we keep the code: it is your account id. When you sign in on another device with the same address, the code puts that device in the same account. It also lets us ban an address that is used for abuse, and keeps a banned address banned. The code is kept until you delete the account, or until the 3-year purge below. For a banned account it is kept after deletion.

End-to-end encryption

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Each device makes its own keys, and the private keys stay on the devices. A message is encrypted on the sending device for the receiving device, so our relay server sees only encrypted data (ciphertext). We do not store message content or pairing fingerprints.

What our relay server stores

Our relay server keeps, until removal:

  • Per account: the account id (the one-way code), a banned flag, the created time, and the last-active month.
  • Per device: public keys, push token or endpoint, allow and deny lists, encrypted name entries, created time, platform, paused flag, and pending approve messages (end-to-end encrypted, kept until the other device is removed, denied or withdrawn).

An account with no send and no added device for about 3 years is deleted with its devices (banned accounts are kept). This also removes an account that has no devices left.

What stays on your device

  • The address you signed in with, shown as Signed in as (a Hide My Email relay address if you chose one), so you can see which address to use on your other devices.
  • Your history, deleted automatically after the number of days you set (default 30). History is device-local and not backed up.
  • Your keys.

Lock screen: message content follows the platform setting (Android sensitive notification content, iPhone Show Previews) or the in-app switch Show message content on lock screen. With Send messages to watch off, Android message notifications are local only (not mirrored to paired wearables). Both switches work per device too. Always hide content (off by default) shows no message content in any notice.

Tracking

No analytics, no ads, no tracking. This site has no third-party scripts, except Cloudflare's bot check on the sign-in and delete-account pages. We set no cookies; Cloudflare security features may set a short-lived one.

Background transmission (Firefox)

The Firefox add-on keeps a push connection to Mozilla (autopush WebSocket) in the background. As a fallback, a background script on pusharmor.com (service worker) receives encrypted messages and forwards them to the add-on inside the browser. Both carry encrypted data only.

Deletion

Use Delete account in the app or the web flow at pusharmor.com/delete-account. Deletion removes the account and all its devices at once. Kept: the one-way code of the address with its ban flag, for banned accounts only, so a banned address stays banned; undelivered pushes at the vendors, up to 7 days (pairing requests up to 4 weeks); copies on your other devices.

Remove on a device deletes that one device only. The account (the one-way code, created time and last-active month) stays until you delete the account, or until the 3-year purge above.

Children

PushArmor is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly hold an account for a child under 13.

Privacy requests

You can delete your account and all its devices yourself: use Delete account in the app, or the web flow at https://pusharmor.com/delete-account. For help with the apps, see the support page.
