Privacy policy

PushArmor is operated by the PushArmor project at pusharmor.com. Contact: support@pusharmor.com.

We never sell or share your data with anyone for their own purposes; the services below process it for us.

Services we use

  • AWS SES: used for email sign-in only, to send the sign-in code. It sees your email address at send time. If a code email bounces, the mail provider keeps that address on a suppression list until we clear it.
  • Google FCM (Android), Apple APNs (iPhone), Google and Mozilla web push (Chrome, Firefox), and a UnifiedPush distributor if you chose one: used to wake your device. They see the push token or endpoint and the encrypted payload only. They hold undelivered messages up to 7 days (pairing requests up to 4 weeks).
  • Cloudflare: runs our server code and database, so it can technically see everything the server sees: your email address briefly at sign-in, your IP address, peer public keys, push tokens and encrypted messages. Cloudflare also keeps our delivery logs for 7 days: account id, peer ids and send results; no content, no IP, no email. Turnstile runs on the email sign-in page and on the delete-account page only.
  • Google and Apple sign-in (if you choose them): used to prove the account. We keep only the keyed hash.

Your email address

We do not store your email address. We keep only a keyed one-way hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the address.

End-to-end encryption

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Each peer makes its own keys on the device, and the private keys stay on the devices. A message is encrypted on the sending peer for the receiving peer, so our server sees ciphertext only. We do not store message content or pairing fingerprints.

What our server stores

The server database keeps, until removal:

  • Per account: the account id (the keyed hash), a banned flag, the created time, and the last-active month (updated at most once per 30 days, by sending or adding a peer).
  • Per peer: public keys, push token or endpoint, allow and deny lists, encrypted name entries, created time, platform, paused flag, and pending approve messages (end-to-end encrypted, up to 9 per peer, kept until the other peer is removed, denied or withdrawn, or a newer approve replaces them).

An account with no send and no added peer for about 3 years is deleted with its peers (banned accounts are kept). This also removes an account that has no peers left.

What stays on your device

  • The address you signed in with, shown as Signed in as (a Hide My Email relay address if you chose one).
  • Your history, deleted automatically after the number of days you set (default 30). History is device-local and not backed up.
  • Your keys.

Lock screen: message content follows the platform setting (Android sensitive notification content, iPhone Show Previews) or the in-app switch Show message content on lock screen. With Send messages to watch off, Android message notifications are local only (not mirrored to paired wearables). Both switches work per peer too. Always hide content (off by default) shows no message content in any notice.

Tracking

No analytics, no ads, no tracking. This site has no third-party scripts, except Turnstile on the sign-in and delete-account pages. We set no cookies; Cloudflare security features may set a short-lived one.

Background transmission (Firefox)

The Firefox add-on keeps a push connection to Mozilla (autopush WebSocket) in the background. As a fallback, a service worker on pusharmor.com receives encrypted messages and forwards them to the add-on inside the browser. Both carry ciphertext only.

Deletion

Use Delete account in the app or the web flow at pusharmor.com/delete-account. Deletion removes the account and all its peer rows at once. Kept: the keyed account hash with its ban flag, for banned accounts only; undelivered pushes at the vendors, up to 7 days (pairing requests up to 4 weeks); copies on your other peers.

Remove on a peer deletes that one peer row only. The account row (the keyed hash, created time and last-active month) stays until you delete the account, or until the 3-year purge above.
