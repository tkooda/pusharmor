About PushArmor

PushArmor sends URLs and text between your own devices. Every message is end-to-end encrypted, so only your devices can read it.

How your messages stay private

Messages are end-to-end encrypted on the device that sends them. Only the devices you paired can open them. The security page shows who sees what.
