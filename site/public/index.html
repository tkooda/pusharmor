Send links and notes between your own devices, always end-to-end encrypted.

PushArmor runs on Android, iPhone, Chrome, and Firefox. Pair your devices once, then send a link or a short note from one to another, or to all of them.

Get PushArmor How it works

Free and open source. Coming soon to the app stores. See the apps.

End-to-end encrypted

Each device makes its own keys, and the private keys never leave it. All of your data is always end-to-end encrypted, and can never be read by the server or anyone else.

Your own devices only

Messages can only ever be read by the devices you paired on your own account. You pair each new device once, by checking that both screens show the same short fingerprint. There are no contacts and no strangers.

Free and open source

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We will never sell or share your data. The full source code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
