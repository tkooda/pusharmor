Send links and short text between your own devices, end-to-end encrypted.

PushArmor runs on Android, iPhone, Chrome and Firefox. Each app or add-on is a peer of your account. Pair your peers once, then send a link or a note from one peer to another, or to all of them.

End-to-end encryption in one screen

  • Each peer makes its own keys. The private keys never leave the device.
  • Before two peers trust each other, you compare a 4-character pairing fingerprint on both screens. If it matches, the pairing is genuine.
  • Each message is encrypted on the sending peer for the receiving peer and signed by the sender. Only the receiving peer can decrypt it.
  • Our server (a Cloudflare Worker) and the push services only carry ciphertext. They cannot read your messages. Your peers check every signature and drop old or repeated messages.
  • We do not store your email address, only a keyed one-way hash of it.
