PushArmor sends links and short notes between your own devices.

Share a page from your browser to your phone, or send a note from your phone to your computer. It works between any devices you pair, and it stays private.

- All of your data is always end-to-end encrypted, and can never be read by the server or anyone else.
- Each device makes its own keys. Our relay only ever sees encrypted data.
- Your devices only. Messages can only ever be read by the devices you paired.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- Free and open source.

Works with the PushArmor apps for Android and iPhone, and the PushArmor browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Sign in with Google, Apple, or an email code. Pair a new device once by checking that both screens show the same short code.
