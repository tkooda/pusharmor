PushArmor sends URLs, short notes, and small files between your own devices, always end-to-end encrypted, for free, with no ads.

Share a URL from your phone to your browser, a phone number from your browser to your phone, or any small file between them. It works between any devices you pair in your account, and it stays private.

- All of your data is always end-to-end encrypted, and can never be read by the server or anyone else.
- Each device makes its own private keys which never leave your device. Our relay server only ever sees encrypted data.
- Your devices only. Messages can only ever be read by the devices you paired.
- Your information is never sold or shared with any 3rd party.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- Free and open source.

Works with the PushArmor apps for Android, iPhone, Chrome, and Firefox.

Log in with Google, Apple, or an email code. Pair a new device once by checking that both screens show the same short code.
