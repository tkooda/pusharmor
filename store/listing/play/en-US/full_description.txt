PushArmor sends links and short text between your own devices.

Share a page from your browser and it shows up on your phone. Send a note
from your phone to your computer. That is all it does, and it does it
privately.

- End-to-end encrypted. Each device makes its own keys. Our relay sees only
  scrambled data.
- Your devices only. Messages go only to devices you paired on your account.
- Links and text only. No files.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- Free and open source.

Works with the PushArmor browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Sign in with Google, Apple, or an email code. Pair a new device once by
checking that both screens show the same short code.
