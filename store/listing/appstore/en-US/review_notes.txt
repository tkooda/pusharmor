Sign-in is required. Tap "Sign in with Google" and use the account above,
or use Sign in with Apple with your own Apple ID. Allow notifications.
To send and receive, a second device on the same account is needed: install
the PushArmor extension in Chrome on a computer, sign in with the same
account, then on both screens tap Pair and check that both show the same
4-character code. Then share any link or text (Share > PushArmor) and it
appears on the other device, and the reverse.
The app sends links and text only, never files. Content is end-to-end
encrypted. It uses only standard encryption (export compliance set).
Delete account: Settings > Delete account.
