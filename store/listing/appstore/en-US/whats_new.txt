PushArmor now runs on iPad. Send small files to your own devices, end-to-end encrypted, from the app or the share sheet. Each device now shows its device ID and the date it was added, so devices with the same name are easy to tell apart. The device list groups devices by pairing status. The Delete messages older than setting now offers 1, 7, 14, 30, 90, 180, and 365 days.
